POWERING ON safety sequence

  1. Secure the robot and confirm it is disabled.
  2. Look around the work area.
  3. Call out “POWERING ON!”
  4. Give everyone time to step away.
  5. Visually confirm the area is clear.
  6. Press PLAY only when it is safe.
