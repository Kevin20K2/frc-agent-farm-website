Get Started

Get your team started.

Six steps from opening PitCrew to a documented season plan. Most teams are through all six in one sitting.

Get Your Team Started →
1

Launch PitCrew

Head to www.the-pitcrew.app and sign in.

2

Create or join your team

Coaches create a new team. Students join with an invite link or code shared by a coach or mentor.

3

Open the Kickoff Wizard

This is the best first experience for a new team — it's the single tool that sets up everything else.

4

Add your team constraints

Team size, tools, budget, experience level. This is what keeps the Wizard's strategies realistic instead of generic.

5

Run the Kickoff session

Work through game notes, strategy scoring, your Minimum Competitive Robot, and an initial build schedule — as a team, in one sitting.

6

Continue into planning and build tools

Your Kickoff session feeds the MoSCoW Board, CAD Board, and Build Calendar automatically. From here, ask Commander anything as you build.

Start Your Kickoff Session

No credit card, no lengthy setup — just your team and a game to plan for.
