Why PitCrew

Turn build-season chaos into a clear team system.

Small teams do not need another generic chatbot. They need a repeatable way to move from discussion to decisions, from decisions to tasks, and from tasks to documented learning.

🧭

Plan with confidence

Capture kickoff notes, team constraints, strategy options, and design tradeoffs in one place.

✅

Turn ideas into task lists, checklists, owners, priorities, and build-season milestones.

📘

How It Works

From kickoff notes to an organized build plan.

PitCrew helps students and mentors ask better questions, make clearer decisions, and leave every meeting with useful next steps.

Capture the game

Paste notes, manual summaries, video takeaways, or team observations.

Add constraints

Tell PitCrew about your team size, tools, parts, budget, programming skill, and event timeline.

Choose priorities

Build a practical strategy board that separates must-build work from nice-to-have ideas.

Execute the plan

Create prototype tasks, CAD requests, checklists, schedules, and notebook entries.

For Teams

Built for the teams that need structure most.

PitCrew is designed for mentors and student leaders trying to keep a robotics season moving with limited time, people, and resources.

🔧

Small FRC Teams

Give a 5–12 student team a repeatable process for strategy, prototype decisions, CAD handoffs, and documentation.

🤖

FTC Teams

Organize parts, tools, programming ability, driver practice, judging notes, and meet deadlines around the team’s real constraints.

🧭

Rebuilding Programs

Help new students and new mentors understand what to do next without turning AI into a replacement for learning.
