The Problem

Small teams do not need more noise. They need a better system.

Kickoff and build season create too many decisions at once: strategy, prototypes, CAD, wiring, software, fabrication, drive practice, judging, and documentation.

⏱️

Limited Time

Build season moves fast. Teams need to turn discussion into action before momentum is lost.

👥

Limited People

Small and rebuilding teams need a process that fits their actual students, mentors, tools, and budget.

📓

Too Much to Track

Tasks, checklists, design decisions, prototype results, and notebook entries can fall through the cracks.

What PitCrew Helps You Do

Turn team conversations into organized build-season work.

Students and mentors ask questions in plain English. PitCrew helps convert those conversations into outputs the team can review, edit, assign, and act on.

🎯

Plan with Confidence

Turn kickoff decisions into clear strategy notes, goals, priorities, and milestones.

🧪

Prototype Smarter

Capture assumptions, tests, risks, results, and next steps before moving into CAD.

📐

Organize Build Work

Create CAD requests, fabrication tasks, build schedules, and ownership in one place.

📓

Document Everything

Keep notes, decisions, photos, test results, and student reflections ready for review.

For Teams

Built for the teams that need structure the most.

PitCrew is designed for mentors, coaches, and student leaders who want a practical system for planning, building, testing, and documenting.

🤖

FTC Teams

Organize parts, tools, programming ability, driver practice, judging notes, and meet deadlines around the team’s real constraints.

Rebuilding Programs
