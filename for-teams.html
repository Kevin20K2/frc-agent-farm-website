For Teams

Built for the teams that need structure most.

Small FRC teams. FTC teams. Rebuilding programs. Teams with one mentor and not enough hours. PitCrew is mentor-assist, not mentor replacement — the AI drafts, your team decides.

🔧 Small FRC teams 🤖 FTC teams 🧭 Rebuilding programs ⏱️ Limited mentor bandwidth
Three Roles, One System

Everyone gets something different out of it.

Coaches

Program-level visibility.

See every subsystem, every deadline, and every student's workload from one dashboard — without chasing updates in five different group chats.

  • Bulk-invite a whole roster in one step
  • Documentation that's ready when judges ask for it
  • A process that survives roster turnover season to season
Mentors

Decision support, not repetition.

Stop re-explaining the same wiring rule or rulebook citation to the third student who asks this week. Let Commander handle the first pass; spend your time on the judgment calls that actually need a human.

  • Specialist-level answers, available any time you're not
  • You still review, approve, and teach — nothing ships unreviewed
  • More time for the parts of mentoring only a person can do
Students

Real ownership, real understanding.

The AI drafts a design or a piece of code — you decide whether it's right, why it works, and what to do if it doesn't. That decision is still yours.

  • Ask Commander anything, any time, without judgment
  • A notebook entry that writes itself into something worth reading
  • Structure without a mentor standing over your shoulder
Mentor-assist, not mentor replacement

PitCrew's agents can draft a mechanism, a block of code, or a first pass at a strategy — but they can't turn a wrench, and they don't make the final call. Every design, every line of code, and every decision still has to be understood and owned by a student. That's not a limitation we're working around; it's the whole point of a student program.

Build-Season Workflow

From Kickoff Day to a shared plan the whole team can see.

Documentation that keeps up with the season.

Commander sessions can create dated engineering-notebook entries that preserve the question, specialists consulted, and recommendations for the team to review.

  • MoSCoW Board keeps priorities visible to the whole team
  • Prototype and CAD requests trace back to the original decision
  • A running record your team can build on through competition prep — not assembled the night before
Competition-Day Workflow

The same structure, at the event.

Ready for the pit, not just the shop.

A Pit Checklist that's actually been used all season, and a Driver Practice log that shows real improvement — not a scramble the morning of your event.

  • Hardware, software, and safety checks in one list
  • Every practice session logged — focus area, duration, self-rating
  • Drive team walks in with data, not just instinct
