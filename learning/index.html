Teaching model

See it. Discuss it. Build it.

01

Watch

Use a short visual lesson to introduce one programming idea without overwhelming new students.

02

Pause and discuss

Stop at the teacher prompts, ask students to predict what happens next, and check understanding.

03

Build and test

Students complete the guided code or hands-on activity, then explain the result in their own words.

Robot lessons always include the POWERING ON sequence

Secure the robot, confirm it is disabled, clear the work area, call “POWERING ON!”, wait for everyone to step away, visually confirm the area is clear, and only then enable the robot.
