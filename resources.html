Why some guides say "Being Updated"

PitCrew's Production app has changed significantly — new sign-in, bulk invites, Team Constraints, and more. Our written guides haven't all caught up yet. Until they do, the fastest accurate path is the app itself: launch PitCrew directly and follow the in-app flow. A new Quick Start guide reflecting the current app is in progress and will become the primary onboarding resource.
