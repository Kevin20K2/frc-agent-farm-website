How Commander Works

One question in. One answer out.

You don't pick an agent, and you don't know in advance who's going to answer. Commander reads your question, decides who's relevant, and synthesizes what comes back.

💬

1. Ask

Type a question in plain language — a rules question, a design problem, a scouting request, anything about your season.

🧭

2. Route

Commander determines which specialists are relevant to your question and brings them in — you can watch them work in real time.

📋

3. Synthesize

Commander combines what each specialist contributed into one coherent, readable answer — not a pile of separate replies to sort through.
